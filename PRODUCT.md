# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Estonian homeowners, developers, and property owners in rural or off-grid areas who need certified, reliable, and ecologically sound wastewater/sewage treatment systems (like BioDisc, Bioficient, BioAir) or rainwater harvesting (Rainstore) solutions.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Kingspan's advanced, certified biological wastewater treatment solutions in Estonia. The site helps users choose the right system via a calculator, learn about Estonian rural subsidies (Hajaasustuse programm), view technical documents/brochures, and submit consultation leads.

## Brand Personality
- Professional: authoritative, certified engineering, reliable.
- Ecological: green-minded, sustainability-focused, clean.
- Premium: high-end brand visuals, clean layouts, crisp typography, trust-evoking colors (Kingspan Blue & Gold).

## Anti-references
- Low-budget plumbing/sewage service directory look.
- Overly cluttered pages with flashing banners or spammy contact popups.
- Generic template websites with poor contrast or messy type scales.

## Design Principles
- Engineering Trust: Present complex technical specs cleanly and confidently.
- Nature-Harmonious Premium: Use clean white spaces, rich navy/slate backgrounds, and subtle gold highlights to feel both premium and ecological.
- Absolute Clarity: Make local bilingual translations (EE/EN) flawless and information like calculator results easy to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance (especially color contrast for dark blue on white/cloud backgrounds).
- Screen-reader friendly navigation and forms.
- Accessible target areas for mobile touch screens.
